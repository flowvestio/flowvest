## What is Flowvest

Flowvest is a simple on-chain protocol for scheduled stablecoin payments.

Instead of relying on reminders, trust, or manual transfers, Flowvest lets users lock funds into a vesting schedule and release them gradually over time.

It is designed for practical payment agreements that need to be simple, transparent, and predictable.

---

## Why Flowvest

Many real-world payment arrangements are repetitive but informal.

Examples include:

-	family support
-	freelancer retainers
-	milestone-based payouts
-	personal financial agreements

These arrangements often depend on trust and manual execution.

Flowvest replaces that with on-chain payment schedules.

---

## How it works

### 1. Lock funds

The owner deposits USDC into a vest schedule.

### 2. Define the schedule

The owner sets:

-	monthly amount
-	duration
-	beneficiary

### 3. Release over time

Funds unlock by period according to the schedule.

### 4. Claim anytime

The beneficiary claims released funds when available.

---

## Core principles

Flowvest is built around a few simple rules:

-	funds are locked upfront
-	release follows fixed intervals
-	all vest data is on-chain
-	no hidden custody
-	no off-chain enforcement needed

The goal is not to create a complex financial system.

The goal is to make recurring payments programmable and transparent.

---

## Fees

Flowvest uses a simple fee model.

-	Create Vest → 0.5% of principal
-	Beneficiary Change → first change free, then 2 USDC
-	Claim / Release → free
-	Terminate → free

All fees are displayed before signing.

---

## Security and transparency

All vest schedules are visible on-chain.

Users can verify:

-	vest parameters
-	claim history
-	termination history
-	beneficiary changes

Flowvest Explorer provides a simple interface for viewing these records.
